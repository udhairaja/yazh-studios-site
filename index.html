About Yazh Studios

From an individual vision to a studio built for brands.

Founded by Udhayakumar P, a video editor and content creator with 6+ years of experience across Tamil television, production houses, and independent projects, Yazh Studios has worked with 100+ clients worldwide , including VJ Siddhu Vlogs, Blacksheep Tamil, Häagen-Dazs Chennai, Femi9 by Nayanthara, REMAX Community, and Sree Kumaran Thangamaligai.

Today, Yazh Studios brings together video editing, content strategy, and digital marketing under one roof , creating content that is clear, creative, consistent, and built for the platform it lives on.
